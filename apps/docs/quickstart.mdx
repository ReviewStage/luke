---
title: "Quickstart"
description: "Start a task, answer your agent, and merge its pull request."
---

Before you start, connect an agent and GitHub. Setup asks for both. You can also connect them in **Settings › Agents** and **Settings › Workspace**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a task">
    Press <kbd>⌘N</kbd> or click **New task**. Choose a repository, describe the change, and send it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the agent work">
    The task opens on its agent's tab, which shows each step the agent takes. The task's plan is on its **Overview** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer in the Inbox">
    When the agent needs something from you, it posts a thread to your Inbox (<kbd>⇧⌘I</kbd>). A thread can end in a decision to make, a to-do, or a short form. Press <kbd>1</kbd>–<kbd>9</kbd> to choose an option, or <kbd>R</kbd> to reply.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the pull request">
    The agent opens a pull request as you. Open the task's **Pull request** tab to read its conversation, checks, and changed files, or open **Chapters** for an outline of the diff.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Merge">
    Merge from the **Pull request** tab. If a check fails, the agent reads why, pushes a fix, and waits for the checks again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  To steer an agent while it works, send it another message from its tab.
</Tip>
