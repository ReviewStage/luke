## Mac app

The Mac app needs Apple silicon and macOS 14 or newer.

1. Download the DMG from the [latest release](https://github.com/ReviewStage/vespin/releases/latest).
2. Open it and drag Vespin to Applications.
3. Open Vespin from Applications.

## Web app

Open [vespin.dev/app](https://vespin.dev/app). On a phone, add it to your home screen.

## Sign in

Sign in with Google or GitHub. A new account then goes through setup:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Welcome">
    Choose **Just me** or **With my team**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a team workspace">
    Only if you chose **With my team**. Name the workspace and invite your teammates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect an agent">
    Connect Claude or Codex, on a plan or an API key. See [Models and who pays](/models).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect GitHub">
    Choose how agents reach your repositories. You can skip this step and connect GitHub later. See [Connect GitHub](/connect-github).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your tools">
    Connect the services agents can use, such as Linear, Notion, or Sentry. You can connect more later in **Settings › Integrations**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If a teammate invited you to their workspace, setup asks you only to connect an agent.
