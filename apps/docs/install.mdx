Vespin runs as a Mac app and as a web app. Both sign in to the same account and show the same workspace.

## Requirements

- **Mac app**: Apple silicon and macOS 14 or newer.
- **Web app**: a current browser. On a phone, add [vespin.dev/app](https://vespin.dev/app) to your home screen.
- **Sign-in**: a Google or GitHub account.
- **Agents**: a Claude or ChatGPT plan, or an Anthropic or OpenAI API key. See [Models and who pays](/models).

## Install the Mac app

1. Download the DMG from the [latest release](https://github.com/ReviewStage/vespin/releases/latest).
2. Open it and drag Vespin to Applications.
3. Open Vespin from Applications. It downloads new versions in the background, and **Settings › About** says when one is ready to install.

## Sign in and set up

Sign in with Google or GitHub. A new account then goes through setup, one card per step:

1. **Welcome**: choose **Just me** or **With my team**.
2. **Create a team workspace**: team accounts only. Name the workspace and invite teammates by email.
3. **Connect an agent**: connect Claude or Codex. See [Models and who pays](/models).
4. **Connect GitHub**: choose how agents reach your repositories, or skip it for now. See [Connect GitHub](/connect-github).
5. **Connect your tools**: connect services agents can use, such as Linear, Notion, or Sentry. You can add more later in **Settings › Integrations**.

If a teammate invited you to their workspace, setup asks only for an agent.
